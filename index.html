Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about TRC DEX trading, liquidity, security, taxes, and investor protection.

What is TRC DEX?

TRC DEX is a decentralized trading platform built on Polygon that allows users to buy and sell TRC directly using POL or USDT through a secure on-chain smart contract.

How can I buy TRC?

You can purchase TRC instantly using either POL or USDT. Simply connect your Web3 wallet, choose your preferred trading pair, and complete the transaction directly on the blockchain.

Which wallets are supported?

TRC DEX supports MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, and most WalletConnect-compatible Web3 wallets on the Polygon network.

Is my investment secure?

Yes. All transactions are executed by an immutable smart contract. Your funds always remain under your control, and every transaction is fully transparent on PolygonScan.

How does TRC pricing work?

TRC uses a dynamic pricing system. Token price automatically increases after buys and adjusts after sells, helping create sustainable long-term growth while rewarding early participants.

What are the sell limits?

To protect liquidity and prevent market dumping, each wallet can sell up to 1% of its current TRC balance once every 24 hours.

How does TRC support small holders?

TRC is designed to benefit every investor. Wallets holding less than 1 TRC can still sell a minimum of 0.01 TRC every 24 hours. This ensures small holders always have access to liquidity and profit opportunities. While larger holders naturally enjoy greater selling power due to larger balances, TRC maintains a fair system that rewards both small and large participants.

Are there buy or sell taxes?

Buy transactions have no direct sell-side deduction, while sell transactions include a 10% tax. These funds strengthen ecosystem reserves, liquidity, and project sustainability.

How is liquidity protected?

TRC DEX separates liquidity from tax reserves. This ensures smooth trading operations while maintaining dedicated funds for platform growth and stability.

Can I trade using both POL and USDT?

Absolutely. TRC supports dual trading pairs, allowing investors to buy and sell seamlessly using either POL or USDT based on preference.

Why invest in TRC?

TRC offers strong long-term growth potential through transparent smart contracts, protected liquidity, increasing token demand, and a secure trading ecosystem. Early investors can benefit from future price appreciation as the community and platform continue to expand.
