Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about TRC DEX trading, liquidity, security, taxes, and investor protection.

What is TRC DEX?

TRC DEX is a decentralized trading platform built on Polygon that allows users to buy and sell TRC directly using POL or USDT through a secure on-chain smart contract.

How can I buy TRC?

You can purchase TRC instantly using either POL or USDT. Simply connect your Web3 wallet, choose your preferred trading pair, and complete the transaction directly on the blockchain.

Which wallets are supported?

TRC DEX supports MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, and most WalletConnect-compatible Web3 wallets on the Polygon network.

Is my investment secure?

Yes. All transactions are executed by an immutable smart contract. Your funds always remain under your control, and every transaction is fully transparent on PolygonScan.

How does TRC pricing work?

TRC uses a dynamic pricing system. Token price automatically increases after buys and adjusts after sells, helping create sustainable long-term growth while rewarding early participants.

What are the Sell limits

To maintain market stability and protect long-term investors, wallets holding more than 1000 TRC may sell up to 1% of their current TRC balance every 24 hours. Only one sell transaction is permitted during each 24-hour period, calculated from the wallet's most recent sell transaction.

This mechanism helps prevent large-scale dumping, supports healthy liquidity, reduces sudden price volatility, and creates a more secure trading environment for all TRC holders.

How does TRC support small holders?

TRC is designed to benefit every investor. Wallets holding less than 1000 TRC can still sell a minimum of 10 TRC every 24 hours. This ensures small holders always have access to liquidity and profit opportunities. While larger holders naturally enjoy greater selling power due to larger balances, TRC maintains a fair system that rewards both small and large participants.

TRC Buy and Sell Tax Structure

TRC uses a transparent and sustainable transaction tax model. Buy transactions include a 1% tax, while sell transactions include a 2% tax.

For investors who both buy and later sell, the combined effective transaction cost is approximately 3%. These taxes are strategically allocated to strengthen liquidity, support TRC buybacks, reduce selling pressure, and fund ecosystem development.

This structure helps maintain long-term price stability, healthy liquidity, and sustainable growth for the entire TRC ecosystem.

TRC Sell Tax Allocation Explained

Every transaction tax collected by TRC DEX is strategically allocated to strengthen the ecosystem and protect investors.

50% of all tax collected remains permanently in the contract as liquidity reserves. This ensures smooth buying and selling, even during periods of high trading activity.

20% is used for periodic TRC buybacks. These buybacks help absorb market selling pressure, improve price stability, and support long-term token value.

20% is allocated to ecosystem development, including marketing, platform expansion, security, partnerships, and community growth.

10% is allocated to the Project Team and Operations, supporting team compensation, technical development, platform maintenance,

This balanced allocation ensures that the majority of taxes directly benefit liquidity and token stability, while also funding the continued growth of the TRC ecosystem.

Can I trade using both POL and USDT?

Absolutely. TRC supports dual trading pairs, allowing investors to buy and sell seamlessly using either POL or USDT based on preference.

Why invest in TRC?

TRC offers strong long-term growth potential through transparent smart contracts, protected liquidity, increasing token demand, and a secure trading ecosystem. Early investors can benefit from future price appreciation as the community and platform continue to expand.
